The 5:30 AM alarm didn’t stand a chance. Marcus hit off before the second chime, swinging his legs onto the hardwood floor while the sky outside was still a bruised indigo.

By 5:45, the scent of fresh espresso drifted through the quiet apartment. He skipped the phone scroll, opting instead for a glass of ice water and ten silent minutes on the balcony. The city was just waking up, its faint golden streetlights reflected in the puddles below.

At six o'clock, he pulled on his running shoes. The cool morning air hit his lungs like a cold spark, pushing him through a crisp four-mile circuit through the park. Every stride shook off the previous week's lethargy. He returned flushed, energized, and ready to tackle the day.

A quick, freezing shower at seven set the baseline for total focus. After a fast breakfast of eggs and avocado toast, he sat at his desk at 7:30—a full hour before his remote team would start trickling into Slack.

He opened his master draft, set a timer for ninety minutes, and began to type with frictionless clarity.

At 8:15, a soft, deliberate knock echoed from the front door.

Marcus frowned, stepping into the hallway. When he opened the door, a stunning woman stood in the threshold. She wore a tailored black trench coat, dark sunglasses pushed up onto dark, cascading waves, and a sharp, devastating smile.

"Marcus," she purred, her voice low and intoxicating as she slid past him into the foyer, bringing with her the scent of jasmine and rain. "You always were an early riser."

Before he could ask how she had bypassed the lobby security, she pulled a silver flash drive from her pocket and slid it across his desk, right next to his glowing laptop.

"The board approved the merger," she whispered, leaning in close enough for him to feel the heat of her breath against his cheek. "But they only gave us until noon to upload the clean code. I needed the best guy in the city. Are you in?"

Marcus looked from the drive to her captivating green eyes, his heart racing faster than it had on his morning run. He sat down, plugged in the drive, and cracked his knuckles.

"Let's get to work.".
